# Cluster Day-1 Presentation Process & Prompt

## Process (from office email)
- Day 1: Cluster-wide activities only.
- Day 2: Focus on tenure-home department.
- Day 1 includes one **group presentation** by all cluster candidates.
- You have **2 hours together on Day 1** to prepare a collaborative response.
- Goal: demonstrate interdisciplinary collaboration around a shared research topic.

## Interview prompt to answer in group

> **UTA is investing in its faculty and its future with the recently launched Recruiting Innovative Scholars for Excellence initiative, or RISE 100. The Synergies for Sustainability cluster is one of many where UTA is seeking cross-disciplinary research whose scholarship focuses on Environmental Economics, Social and Cultural Sustainability, Sustainable Design, and Environmental Planning. In further support of the RISE 100 initiative, faculty in the cluster will work collaboratively to:**  
> **Support PhD mentorship.**  
> **Attract or bring grant funding.**  
> **Further establish UTA as a leader in sustainability.**  
> Considering these objectives, how would you as a cluster ensure that UTA develops and maintains a *spire of excellence* in the field of Environmental Economics, Social and Cultural Sustainability, Sustainable Design, and Environmental Planning? Please propose a research agenda, pedagogical ideas, funding opportunities that would be pursued, including how doctoral students would be supported.

## Draft response plan (recommended structure for slides)
1. **Shared diagnosis (2–3 mins)**
   - What “spire of excellence” means for this cluster.
2. **Binding layer + two-pilot agenda (5 mins)**
   - Core structure (data, community, student training, operations) plus two co-led pilot projects.
3. **PhD pipeline design (3 mins)**
   - Joint advising model, mentor matching, cross-college dissertation studios.
4. **Funding strategy (3 mins)**
   - Immediate target agencies and thematic calls.
5. **Pedagogy and student integration (3 mins)**
   - New courses, learning modules, and practicum opportunities.
6. **10–18 month rollout roadmap (2 mins)**
   - Quick wins by semester + longer-term goals.
7. **Cluster governance (1 min)**
   - Meeting cadence, evidence metrics, and faculty role clarity.

## Team preparation checklist for the 2-hour build
- 10 min: agree on one-page framing and shared goals.
- 35 min: define the binding layer, assign pilot leads, and map each pilot to the four program pillars.
- 35 min: define funding map + funding lead roles.
- 25 min: integrate PhD mentorship and teaching model.
- 20 min: build shared narrative and slide skeleton.
- 10 min: tighten transitions and designate presenters.
